# Word Entries

> [!Important]
> It is not recommended to add any word entries before setting up both [grammar](/docs/user/initial-setup/grammar.md) and [inflection tables](/docs/user/initial-setup/inflection-tables.md).
> 
> In fact, if your conlang has no word classes (see [Grammar](/docs/user/initial-setup/grammar.md)), then you **cannot add any form or definition data.**

The most complicated part of adding a word entry in Tollerus is inflected word forms. The good news is that if you have already configured your conlang's [inflection tables](/docs/user/initial-setup/inflection-tables.md), then the interface does a good job of guiding you by various prompts and automated functions.

However, it's still a good idea to develop an understanding of the system.
